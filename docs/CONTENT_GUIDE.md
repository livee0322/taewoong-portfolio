# Content guide

## Voice

Write in Korean first. English may clarify titles, labels, roles, or tools but must not replace the information a Korean hiring manager needs. Use a calm, specific, factual tone. Describe the action, the constraint, and the output where known.

The portfolio does not claim that TaeWoong is a developer when the role was product planning or implementation coordination. State the actual boundary: for example, “서비스 요구사항을 정리하고 구현 결과를 검토하는 과정에 AI 기반 개발 도구를 활용했습니다.”

## Preferred construction

`무엇을 정리했는지 → 어떻게 함께 진행했는지 → 무엇을 확인했는지`

Example: “셀러차트 웹과 앱의 화면을 QA하고, 디자인 수정 사항을 반영했습니다. 반복되는 요소는 Figma 컴포넌트로 정리했습니다.”

Avoid vague claims such as “혁신적인 솔루션”, “최적의 결과”, “사용자 중심의 경험”, “시너지”, “새로운 가치”, or “끊임없이 성장”. Do not inflate a small contribution and do not hide useful work behind abstract collaboration language.

Prefer short verbs tied to evidence: `촬영했습니다`, `정리했습니다`, `QA했습니다`, `화면 크기에 맞게 재배치했습니다`. Let strong images carry the proof; captions identify the medium, purpose, or design decision in one sentence and do not repeat what is already visible.

## Content maintenance

- A `VisualWork` entry does not need a description. Title and category are the default; add a one-sentence `description` only when it explains a decision the image itself cannot show. Do not give every card in an archive the same explanatory sentence pattern — it reads as generated copy.
- Summaries answer what the work was and TaeWoong’s contribution in two to four short sentences.
- Role fields use concrete nouns such as `Service planning`, `UX/UI`, `QA`, `Content direction`.
- Process steps describe a visible decision or handoff, not a generic project phase.
- Results only use measurements that are known and reviewable. Otherwise use a specific learning or operational outcome.
- Media alt text identifies the project, object, and relevant state; it is not filename text.
- Career statements follow the source portfolio’s actual work categories. GPA KOREA work is described as SellerChart web/app QA, design revisions, and Figma components; do not relabel it as an app launch or general service planning.

Long narrative material can live under `content/projects/`; keep the route-facing summary and metadata in the typed project record.

## Asset evidence

- Use original production assets only after visually checking the file, its category, and whether it supports the adjacent claim.
- Keep the source-to-site path explicit: original source → selected final asset → optimized local file in `public/images/...`.
- Do not use source-only clip art as a final portfolio result. Exclude private captures, unapproved personal information, and unfinished product-model material.
- A long detail page should appear as a readable crop or a substantial vertical preview, never a decorative thumbnail.
